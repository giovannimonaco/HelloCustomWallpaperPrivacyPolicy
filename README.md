# Hello Custom Wallpapers Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use Hello Custom Wallpapers ("Hello").

## In Short

Hello doesn't collect any data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells, shares or sends your data to anyone, including us.

## Your Wallpapers

Hello makes your wallpapers on your device. It has no servers, and nothing you create is uploaded anywhere.

## Photos

To save a wallpaper, Hello asks only for permission to add photos to your library. With this permission, it can't see, change or delete the photos already there. You can change it at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Hello.

## Your Settings and Widgets

The choices you make in Hello, like your last colors, style and app icon, and the settings of your widgets are stored on your device. They may be included in your device's backups. Deleting Hello deletes them. Wallpapers you've saved stay in your photo library until you delete them.

## Crash Reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, we may receive crash reports and usage statistics. They don't identify you, and we use them only to fix problems and improve Hello. You can turn this off at any time in the same place.

## Third Parties

Hello contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and shares no data with third parties.

## Children

Hello doesn't collect data from anyone, children included.

## Your Rights

Because Hello doesn't collect personal data, we hold none about you to access, correct or delete. Everything Hello makes stays on your device, under your control.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
